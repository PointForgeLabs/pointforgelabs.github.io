Common Questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". This will sync your subscription from the App Store or Google Play.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account. Go to your device's subscription settings to cancel. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Delete Account". This will permanently remove your account and all associated data. If you need help, email us at the address above.

I found incorrect information in the archive.

If you spot an error in Ain't Dere No More, please email us with the place name and correction. We rely on the community to keep the archive accurate.

Something else?

Email us at support@pointforgelabs.com and we'll get back to you within 48 hours.
