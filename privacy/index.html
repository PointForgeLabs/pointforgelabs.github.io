Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

Point Forge Labs ("we", "us", "our") operates the Alchemixt and Ain't Dere No More mobile applications. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. You may also provide an optional username and bio.

User-generated content: Content you submit through our apps, including cocktail recipes, saved drinks, ingredient preferences, place submissions, memories, and photos.

Usage data: We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve our apps, including screen views, feature usage, and crash reports. This data is not linked to your identity.

Purchase information: If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation of your subscription status from these platforms via RevenueCat.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To display your contributions (recipes, memories, photos) within the apps
  • To improve our apps and develop new features
  • To communicate with you about your account or support requests

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS). User-generated content such as recipes and preferences may also be stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

Photos uploaded to our apps are stored in secure cloud storage (Supabase Storage).

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Supabase (database hosting), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Mapbox (map services for Ain't Dere No More). These providers only access data necessary to perform their services.
  • Other users: Content you submit publicly (place entries, memories, shared recipes) is visible to other users of the app.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Access, update, or delete your account information at any time through the app settings
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of analytics collection through your device settings

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Alchemixt contains references to alcoholic beverages and is intended for users of legal drinking age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.
